What Do You Expect to See?

A Quick Prediction Exercise

Before you explore a dataset in Part 2 of this study, we want to understand what your prior beliefs are. You will be shown a series of data attributes, one at a time. We want to understand how you think each variable relates to teenagers who have been diagnosed with depression or anxiety vs. those who have not. For example, you will see attributes such as daily screen time or hours of sleep.

Similar to the tutorial, for each attribute, you will place a set of tokens into bins to represent how common you think each range of values is, separately for teens who have been diagnosed with depression or anxiety and teens who have not. There are no right or wrong answers. We are interested in your honest, personal beliefs about the relationships between these variables.

This is Part 1 of 2 in the study. Once you finish this part, you'll get a verification code — you will need this for the Qualtrics form before moving on to Part 2.
